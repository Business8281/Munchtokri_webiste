Frequently Asked Questions

How are the snacks packaged for shipping? expand_more
We use vacuum-sealed high-barrier pouches for all our fried and roasted snacks. These are then placed in sturdy, triple-layered corrugated boxes with eco-friendly cushioning to prevent any breakage during transit. Our goal is for you to hear that fresh "crunch" as soon as you open the pack.
Do you offer International Shipping? expand_more
Currently, we only ship within India. We are working on international logistics to bring the taste of Karnataka to the world soon. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know when we expand!
What if my order is damaged during transit? expand_more
While we take utmost care, accidents can happen. If you receive a damaged package, please take a photo and contact our support team at support@hurigalu.in within 24 hours of delivery. We will issue a replacement or refund immediately.
