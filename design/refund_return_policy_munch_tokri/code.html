Eligibility for Returns

expand_more

Due to the perishable nature of our products, returns are strictly handled based on hygiene and safety standards. To be eligible for a return:

  • The request must be initiated within 7 days of the delivery date.
  • Items must be unopened, in their original vacuum-sealed or boxed packaging.
  • The product must be in the same condition that you received it.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is mandatory.
warning

Non-Returnable Items

expand_more

Certain situations are exempt from returns:

  • Perishable goods that have been opened or consumed.
  • Items purchased during clearance or flash sales.
  • Gift cards and promotional vouchers.
  • Products damaged by improper storage after delivery.
safety_check

Damaged or Stale Products

expand_more

Our Hurigalu Promise ensures you never pay for quality failure. If your snacks arrive stale or the packaging is compromised during transit:

  1. Take a clear photograph of the damaged item.
  2. Email the photos and your order ID to our support team.
  3. We will ship a replacement free of charge or issue a full refund immediately.
payments

Refund Process

expand_more

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection. If approved:

  • The refund will be processed to the original method of payment.
  • You will receive credit within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank's policy.
  • Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item was damaged upon arrival.
